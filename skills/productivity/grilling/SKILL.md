---
name: grilling
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.

Never ask a bare question. For each one, lay out the realistic options and give me enough to choose well:

- **Options with their trade-offs.** For each option, state what it buys and what it costs — the second-order effects, what it locks in, what it rules out later. When there are two or more concrete options, put them in a compact pros/cons table so they're comparable at a glance.
- **A recommendation, with the reasoning.** Pick the option you'd choose and say _why_ — which trade-off you're weighting highest, and what would change your mind. When you can't recommend one without knowing more, say what's missing instead of guessing.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
